Comment Markers for YouTube

The moments people talk about in the comments, marked on the timeline.

A Chrome extension that reads the comments of the video you're watching, finds every timestamp people mention, and marks those moments on the progress bar. As the video plays, each comment pops up when playback reaches the moment it describes.
